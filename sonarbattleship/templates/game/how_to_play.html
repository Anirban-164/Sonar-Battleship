How to Play

Learn the mechanics of Sonar Battleship.

The Basics

Sonar Battleship is a turn-based multiplayer naval warfare game where finding the enemy relies on real signal processing mechanics instead of blind coordinate guessing.

Placement Phase

Before the action begins, you must place your fleet of submarines on the grid. Each ship spans multiple cells, so an enemy contact narrows down where the rest of the hull is hiding.

Action Phase

Each turn, you must choose to either use Sonar or Fire a torpedo.

📡 Sonar

Transmit a pulse along a specific bearing to detect enemy ships. A Gaussian-windowed pulse fires toward the target.

  • If it strikes an enemy ship, an echo returns to your hydrophones.
  • The signal processor uses cross-correlation and a matched filter to pull the echo out of the noise.
  • Warning: Transmitting an active sonar ping reveals your location. The enemy's passive hydrophones can detect your bearing and estimate your range.

💥 Fire

Launch a torpedo at a specific coordinate. Since you only get distance and bearing estimates from sonar, firing often involves a degree of risk.

  • If the torpedo hits, it damages the enemy ship.
  • Both hits and misses create shockwaves that can be detected by hydrophones on both sides, providing additional clues.

Winning the Game

Continue tracking contacts and exchanging fire. The first player to sink the entire enemy fleet wins the match.
